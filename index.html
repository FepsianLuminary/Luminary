What is the Luminary? (˶˃ ᵕ ˂˶) .ᐟ.ᐟ

The Luminary is a student-led peer tutoring organization for anyone who feels lost, embarrassed, or overwhelmed in class. We match students with trained tutors who meet them where they are and help them rediscover their curiosity.

✦

What is a Lumine? ദ്ദി(˵ •̀ ᴗ - ˵ )

Lumines are the trained peer tutors of the Luminary. They take the time to understand how a student learns best, whether that is seeing it, hearing it, or trying it, and teach to that instead of repeating how the class already explained it.

✦

What is a Spark? ꉂ(˵˃ ᗜ ˂˵)

A Spark is a Lumine's specific strength within their subject, like Geometry or Chemistry. Sparks let students be matched with a tutor who is confident in exactly the topic they need help with.
