What is the Luminary?

The Luminary is a student-led, student-serving peer tutoring organization for learners who feel overwhelmed, embarrassed to ask questions in class, or unsure of how they learn best. We pair students with trained peer tutors who meet them where they are, both academically and personally, and help them find their way back to the curiosity a fast classroom pace can bury.

What is a Lumine?

Lumines are the trained peer tutors of the Luminary — the students who sit down with their peers, figure out how they learn best, and guide them through their lessons in a way that actually clicks.

What is a Spark?

A Spark is a Lumine's specific strength within their subject, like Geometry or Chemistry. Sparks let students be matched with a tutor who is confident in exactly the topic they need help with.

Visual Learner

Learns best through seeing information — images, diagrams, written text.

Signs you're one: you like charts and notes, remember faces better than names, and often think in pictures.

*ੈ✩‧₊˚

Auditory Learner

Learns best through hearing and speaking information — lectures, discussions, audio.

Signs you're one: you remember things you hear better than things you read, like reading aloud, and follow spoken directions easily.

*ੈ✩‧₊˚

Kinesthetic Learner

Learns best through hands-on activity and movement — doing, touching, practicing.

Signs you're one: you fidget or move while thinking, prefer "learning by doing," and struggle sitting through long lectures without a physical outlet.
